## Sources

**Word order and part of speech: Yr Amliadur** (CC BY-SA 4.0)

> Knight, D., Morris, S., Tovey-Walsh, B., Fitzpatrick, T. and Anthony, L. (2020). *Yr Amliadur: Frequency lists for contemporary Welsh.* Cardiff University. https://doi.org/10.17035/d.2020.0120164107

The words are taken from its lemma list. A word's `rank` is the lemma's rank in that list.

**English meanings: Wiktionary** (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Meanings come from the English Wiktionary's Welsh entries, written by Wiktionary's contributors (https://en.wiktionary.org/), by way of the machine-readable extract at kaikki.org made by Tatu Ylonen's wiktextract (https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Welsh/). The snapshot used was published on 21 October 2026. Meanings were shortened, and some were rewritten by hand.

**Noun gender, capitalisation and word forms: Lecsicon Cymraeg Bangor** (CC0 1.0)

Uned Technolegau Iaith, Prifysgol Bangor. https://github.com/techiaith/lecsicon-cymraeg-bangor, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5211667. Every form in `Forms.csv` comes from it. CC0 needs no credit; it is credited here anyway.
